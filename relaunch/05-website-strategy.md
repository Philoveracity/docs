# 05 — Website Strategy

## Garrett Plane 1 — Strategy

## 1. Website mission

The website should accomplish four things simultaneously:

1. Reintroduce Philoveracity as a technology and research company.
2. Make its philosophy legible without requiring the visitor to read a manifesto.
3. Demonstrate real technologies and human capabilities.
4. Provide a durable institutional home for future research and Works.

## 2. Primary user groups

### Explorer

Interested in ideas, possibility, and unusual technologies.

Needs:
- orientation
- discovery
- compelling ideas
- a reason to continue

### Builder

Interested in technical work.

Needs:
- architecture
- demonstrations
- technical depth
- evidence

### Operator

Interested in using a Philoveracity Work.

Needs:
- clear product understanding
- capability demonstration
- path to product information

### Researcher

Interested in frontier questions.

Needs:
- papers
- experiments
- methodology
- evidence
- current state

### Humanist / Philosopher

Interested in what technology means for human beings.

Needs:
- philosophy
- worldview
- human-machine doctrine
- common-good reasoning

### Collaborator

Potential partner, researcher, founder, engineer, or institution.

Needs:
- credibility
- portfolio
- areas of interest
- evidence of serious work
- contact path

## 3. Strategic objectives

### Objective A — Reposition

A first-time visitor should understand that Philoveracity is no longer primarily a web/design services company.

### Objective B — Establish identity

The site should communicate:

> Love of Truth.

and:

> Teach people to fly.

without feeling like a slogan exercise.

### Objective C — Establish credibility

The site must show actual work, evidence, research, and functioning technologies.

### Objective D — Preserve depth

Visitors who want the deeper philosophy should be able to discover it.

### Objective E — Create institutional runway

The site should remain coherent as the company adds products, research, experiments, and future technologies.

## 4. User success criteria

A successful first visit should allow a visitor to answer:

- What is Philoveracity?
- What does it believe?
- What is it building?
- Why does it build it?
- What has it actually built?
- Where can I explore further?

## 5. Business success criteria

The site should:

- establish the new institutional identity
- support product discovery
- support research publication
- create credibility with technical audiences
- provide a durable portfolio
- create future pathways for collaboration
- avoid reducing the company to one current product

## 6. Strategic narrative

The preferred narrative is:

**Teach people to fly.**

→ technology expands possibility

→ truth precedes invention

→ Philoveracity discovers and tests

→ Philoveracity builds instruments

→ people gain new capabilities

→ technology contributes to human flourishing

## 7. Progressive revelation

The homepage should not expose the entire worldview at once.

Suggested depth:

**Layer 1**
> Teach people to fly.

**Layer 2**
> We build technologies that expand human possibility.

**Layer 3**
> Discover truth. Give it form.

**Layer 4**
> Truth precedes invention.

**Layer 5**
> The human remains human. The machine remains a machine.

**Layer 6**
> Heaven gives meaning. Earth gives expression.

Each deeper layer should be discoverable rather than mandatory.

## 8. "Show, not tell"

A functioning demonstration should generally outperform a paragraph claiming capability.

The site should prioritize:

- demos
- prototypes
- screenshots
- videos
- experiments
- evidence
- actual outcomes

## 9. Strategic constraints

- Keep the existing logo initially.
- Do not make the site look like a generic AI startup.
- Do not over-theologize the homepage.
- Do not bury the technology beneath philosophy.
- Do not reduce Philoveracity to Focusa.
- Do not create excessive navigation.
- Do not build elaborate interaction before information architecture is resolved.
- Do not confuse speculation with established research.

## 10. Success test

After a visitor spends five minutes on the site, they should understand:

> Philoveracity is a company devoted to discovering truth and building technologies that give people new capabilities.

The site should make that understanding feel discovered rather than announced.
