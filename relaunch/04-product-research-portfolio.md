# 04 — Product & Research Portfolio

## 1. Portfolio principle

Philoveracity is larger than any current product.

The portfolio should therefore be organized around **discovery and expression**, not around a flat list of software products.

## 2. Primary branches

### WORKS

**Give truth form.**

Works are technologies that have been built into usable instruments.

Current examples:

- Focusa
- UIAI Engine

Future examples may include:

- agent workforce systems
- voice interfaces
- physical computing
- robotics
- sensing systems
- new human-computer interfaces
- experimental consumer technologies

### RESEARCH

**Discover truth.**

Research explores questions before or alongside productization.

Potential research areas:

- machine cognition
- metacognition
- prediction
- agent intelligence
- human-computer interaction
- embodied intelligence
- robotics
- sensing
- simulation
- computation
- consciousness and related frontier questions
- human-machine interaction
- novel interfaces

## 3. Works content model

Each Work should answer:

1. What is it?
2. What problem or limitation does it address?
3. What becomes possible?
4. What is technically distinctive?
5. What evidence exists?
6. What can someone see or try?
7. What is the current state?
8. What is next?

## 4. Focusa

Focusa should be presented as an instrument for dramatically increasing the agency of an individual operator.

The portfolio question is:

> **What can one person command now?**

Relevant concepts include:

- persistent agent work
- mission cohesion
- Workpoints
- trajectory
- proof
- recovery
- session transfer
- tool use
- orchestration
- workforce coordination
- human direction

The website should show actual capability rather than merely listing architecture.

## 5. UIAI Engine

UIAI Engine should be presented as an instrument that gives agents the ability to operate graphical and computer interfaces.

The portfolio question is:

> **What can an agent physically operate now?**

Again, demonstrations should be central.

## 6. Research entities

### Paper

A formal intellectual artifact.

Potential numbering:

- P001 — Truth Before Discovery
- P002 — The Instrument and the Image
- P003 — Teach People to Fly
- P004 — From Above
- P005 — Giving Intelligence Hands
- P006 — Human Machinery
- P007 — The Common Good in an Age of Artificial Intelligence

These are candidate titles, not yet committed publications.

### Experiment

A bounded investigation.

Format:

**EXP-001**

Each experiment should record:

- question
- hypothesis
- setup
- method
- observation
- result
- interpretation
- limitations
- next step

### Observatory

A living index of the frontier.

Potential states:

- Known
- Observed
- Suspected
- Question
- Experiment
- Emerging
- Disproven
- Crystallized

### We Were Wrong

A correction archive.

This should be treated as a feature of institutional integrity, not a marketing stunt.

## 7. Ideas

Ideas are philosophical and conceptual work that may not yet qualify as formal research.

Potential subjects:

- truth and technology
- human agency
- technology as instrument
- human/machine boundaries
- the meaning of intelligence
- freedom and automation
- the common good
- the relationship between discovery and invention

## 8. Flight Notes

A lighter publication layer.

Potential recurring structure:

**Above** — the idea, principle, or question.

**Below** — the experiment, engineering, evidence, or product.

**Flight** — what capability or understanding emerged between them.

## 9. Experiments and products should connect

A research item can lead to:

**Research → Experiment → Work**

A Work can point back to:

**Work → Evidence → Research**

This makes the portfolio a living graph rather than a marketing catalog.

## 10. Portfolio test

Every Work should ultimately answer:

> **What can someone do now that they couldn't do before?**

This is the central portfolio metric of human capability.

## 11. Future-proofing

The architecture must support products that do not yet exist.

Do not hard-code the site around Focusa and UIAI Engine.

Those are the first visible Works, not the permanent boundary of Philoveracity.
