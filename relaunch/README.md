# Philoveracity Relaunch Initiative

**Status:** Foundational planning  
**Branch:** `relaunch/philoveracity-2026`  
**Date:** 2026-09-27

This directory contains the master planning documents for the relaunch of Philoveracity.com from its historical web/design-services identity into a technology, research, and experimental works company.

## Core

- **Name:** Philoveracity
- **Meaning:** Love of Truth
- **Mission:** Teach people to fly.
- **Operating philosophy:** Discover truth. Give it form.
- **Primary institutional idea:** Build technologies that expand human possibility and contribute to human flourishing.
- **Human-machine boundary:** The human remains human. The machine remains a machine.
- **Metaphysical axiom:** Heaven gives meaning. Earth gives expression.

## Design method

The website will be designed through Jesse James Garrett's five planes:

1. Strategy
2. Scope
3. Structure
4. Skeleton
5. Surface

A final **Expression** pass is added after Surface to verify that the finished experience actually feels like Philoveracity rather than a generic technology company.

## Working rule

Do not jump directly into visual design or implementation. Resolve the institutional vision first, then move through the planes sequentially. The existing Philoveracity logo remains unchanged unless later evidence gives a compelling reason to modify it. The visual system may evolve around it, including a refined red signal color and potentially one secondary highlight color.
